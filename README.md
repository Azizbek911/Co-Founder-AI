# CoFounderAI

CoFounderAI is an AI-powered business assistant designed to help entrepreneurs and small business owners start, manage, and grow their businesses with confidence.

The platform acts as a virtual co-founder by providing practical guidance on every stage of building a business, including:

* Business registration and legal requirements
* Required documents and paperwork
* Startup costs and budget planning
* Tax information and financial obligations
* Business planning and strategy
* Step-by-step instructions on where and how to begin
* Industry-specific recommendations and insights

CoFounderAI aims to make entrepreneurship more accessible by transforming complex regulations, financial information, and business processes into simple, actionable advice. Whether someone is launching their first company or expanding an existing small business, CoFounderAI provides personalized support to help them make informed decisions.

Our mission is to empower small businesses with intelligent guidance, allowing founders to focus on innovation and growth while having an AI co-founder available whenever they need assistance.
